About Me

Hi, I’m Meas Hav. I’ve spent more than six years working in IT Infrastructure, with experience across different industries and business environments. Each new setting has taught me something different, and adapting to new needs, processes, and ways of working is one of the parts of my work I value most.

I like to understand what a business is trying to achieve and what users actually need before suggesting a technical solution. I’m curious about how organizations work, and I enjoy finding ways technology can help people do their work more productively and efficiently. For me, technology matters most when it creates real value for a business—not just when the systems are running.

This portfolio is a personal record of my professional journey, the experiences that have shaped me, and the things I continue to learn as I grow in IT Infrastructure.
